# SUPER EZHAN CAR

**Open in MakeCode Arcade:** https://arcade.makecode.com/#pub:github:ezhanmmd/ezhan-super-car

Or in MakeCode: **Import** → **Import URL** → `https://github.com/ezhanmmd/ezhan-super-car`

Ezhan's own MakeCode Arcade car racer.


## Controls
- A = gas, B = brake (B + turn fast = drift), Left/Right = steer
- UP = nitro (on rocket cars: rocket fly)
- DOWN = fire (the missile car fires in ALL directions)
- B + DOWN = jet hop (jet cars) or horn
- MENU = pause (on the Connect scan screen, MENU = F5 refresh)

Race, Super Grand Prix, Endless Repeat, Time Trial, BOSS, Missile Mayhem, Rocket Rally,

## Features
- Super Ezhan title screen, car login (username + 4-digit game PIN) or Guest
- 17 tracks (14 race tracks + boss fortress + Missile Mayhem arena + secret Rainbow Road)
- Track branches: shortcuts, some behind secret gates
- 10 modes: Race, Super Grand Prix, Endless Repeat, Time Trial, Boss, Missile Mayhem, Rocket Rally, Slime Survival, Jet Jump, Drift King
- 18 cars (3 bonus cars unlocked with golden keys), missile car, jet, rocket, slime, tank, hover cars
- Paint Maker with live preview: body, stripes, wheels, windows and lights
- Jet ramps (you fly), boost pads, slime puddles, lava and ice
- Pop, squish and crunch sounds
- Mega boss with 3 phases, 10 rivals, 10 kinds of weather
- 8 upgrades with 10 levels each, plus lap and race times saved with settings
- Mega Shop: 103,904 items (about 4.2 x 10^70 outfits), all decoded from numbers. You can wear lots at once, and coins are infinite.
- Robot add-ons: robot arms, drone helper, auto-turret and robo-shield
- Agents & Bots helpers: Code & GitHub, gurlagventv, Site Maker, PC Agent and Remi
- Connect menu (WiFi, Bluetooth, Car). It is pretend: the names are made up, it never uses real WiFi or Bluetooth, and you can add your own names.
